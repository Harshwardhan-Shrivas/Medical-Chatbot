# HWS's Medical-Chatbot

## Overview

The **HWS's Medical Chatbot** is an virtual assistant designed to provide users with preliminary medical information, symptom checking, and general health guidance. It leverages natural language processing (NLP) to understand user queries and respond with relevant, medically-reviewed content in real-time.

## Key Features

- 🩺 **Symptom Checker**: Users can input symptoms and receive a possible list of conditions or advice on whether to seek professional help.
- 💬 **Conversational Interface**: Natural and intuitive communication through text, with multilingual support.
- 🕒 **24/7 Availability**: Offers around-the-clock assistance, especially useful in non-emergency situations or for general inquiries.
- 🔒 **Data Privacy**: Designed with user privacy in mind, adhering to healthcare data standards like HIPAA (if applicable).

## Use Cases

- Self-assessment of common symptoms
- Medication information
- First-aid guidance and general wellness tips
- Mental health and stress management
- Support for chronic condition tracking (e.g., diabetes, hypertension)

## Limitations

> ⚠️ This chatbot is not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a licensed healthcare provider for serious health concerns.
